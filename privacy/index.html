Privacy policy: The Living Ledger publishing tool

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers the private publishing tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel The Living Ledger to publish the channel's own videos. The tool is not offered to anyone else.

Use of YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (Google OAuth): uploads the channel's own videos and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, caption tracks and publishing schedule; reads the processing status of those videos and the channel's own analytics (read-only).
  • Google Drive (drive.file scope only): stores backups of the channel's own episode files. It cannot see any other files in the owner's Drive.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not access other users' channels, accounts or data.
  • It does not collect personal information about viewers.
  • It does not post comments, like, subscribe or send messages.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Storage, retention and deletion

OAuth tokens and episode data are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's own Google Drive. Analytics data retrieved from the YouTube Analytics API is kept only as long as needed for the channel's own reporting and is refreshed or deleted within 30 days. The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the tool's local data folder and its Google Drive archive folder.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (select "The Living Ledger" and choose "Remove access"). After revocation the tool can no longer access YouTube or Google Drive.

Contact

The channel owner, via the contact details on The Living Ledger's YouTube channel "About" page.
